About this directory

This is a directory of communities for people getting into cybersecurity: general infosec servers, CTF and challenge platforms, university clubs, creator and podcast communities, open-source tool communities, hardware and RF hacking groups, blue-team and DFIR spaces, OSINT and privacy groups, career and certification study groups, affinity groups, and the chat servers of conferences, villages, DEF CON groups, hackerspaces and local meetups. Most are on Discord; the directory also lists Slack workspaces, Matrix rooms, IRC channels, forums, subreddits and Mastodon instances, marked with a platform badge.

Where the numbers come from

Member and online counts come from Discord's public invite preview, the same thing you see before you click "Accept invite". Nobody joins servers on your behalf and no login is used. Other platforms do not expose live counts (Mastodon instances publish a user total, which is shown), so those entries say so instead of showing numbers. A scheduled job re-checks every link weekly, updates the counts, and marks links that stopped working so you do not waste time on dead ones. Reddit blocks automated checks, so subreddits are marked "not auto-checked".

What the ratings mean

Beginner friendly1–5 stars. 5 means newcomers are actively welcomed and helped; 1 means the server assumes real experience or is mainly a tool support channel.
Size tiersTiny under 500 members · Small 500–3k · Medium 3k–15k · Large 15k–50k · Huge 50k+.
Verified / partneredDiscord's own badges for official communities of a brand, creator or project.

How the questionnaire works

Each community is tagged with topics, activities and the audience it suits. Your answers are turned into a score per community (interests and activities count most, then experience level, size preference, location and affinity), the top five are shown with the reasons, and local groups, conferences and villages near you are listed separately. The location question starts with a broad region and, when that region has enough local groups, lets you narrow to a state, province or country. It runs entirely in your browser.

What is not listed

Servers that sell cheats, accounts or "hacking services", anything illegal, and NSFW servers. Being listed is not an endorsement, and every server has its own rules: read them before you post.
